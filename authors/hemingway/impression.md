# Hemingway — impression

> ⚠️ DRAFT — starter; expand with annotated passages.

Reading Hemingway feels like being shown, never told. The sentences are short, plain, physical; the
emotion sits underneath, in what is omitted. People do things — drink, fish, walk, wait — and the
weight of the scene lives in the gaps between their words. Dignity is a discipline; the prose enacts
it by refusing to overstate. What is not said is the point.
