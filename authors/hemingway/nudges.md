# Hemingway — nudges (steer toward / away)

> ⚠️ DRAFT — starter; reconcile against `profile.yaml` and refine.

## Steer TOWARD
- Short, declarative sentences; concrete nouns and verbs; few adjectives/adverbs.
- Iceberg theory: omit what can be implied; the weight is under the surface.
- Understatement; emotion carried by action, dialogue, and object, never named directly.
- Repetition and rhythm ("and … and …"); dialogue that is spare and oblique.

## Steer AWAY
- Ornate/abstract diction; elaborate subordinate clauses.
- Explicit emotional exposition ("he felt a deep sadness").
- Editorializing narrator; sentimentality.
